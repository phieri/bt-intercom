Conversations with boundaries

Make room for the right voices

Talk groups decide where each headset’s microphone goes. Build a shared workshop, separate crews, or overlapping circles with one headset in more than one group.

01 / MEMBERSHIP

Choose who’s in

Assign paired headsets to one or more groups. When groups are configured, an unassigned headset is kept out of group routes.

02 / ROUTING

Hear only your group

A microphone reaches other headsets sharing at least one group—never its own headset. With no groups configured, the intercom stays all-to-all.

AlexJulesMorgan Jules bridges both groups

Taking turns? In half-duplex mode, each group has its own first-in, first-out queue. Separate groups can have a talker at the same time.

A small terminal, a clear picture

Keep the network in hand

Open bt-intercom panel for the terminal control panel. Check Bluetooth connection and signal, confirm duplex audio is available, then manage groups and membership without editing config files.

  • TabSwitch between groups and headsets
  • ↑ ↓Select a group or member
  • nCreate a talk group
  • dRemove the selected group
  • SpaceToggle headset membership
  • EnterSave a new group name
  • EscCancel name entry or quit the panel

Changes save to your user config and a running intercom picks them up on its next routing update.
