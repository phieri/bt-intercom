HOW AUDIO MOVES 02 / 09

Your voice goes through the host.

Headsets do not connect directly to each other. The Linux host routes your microphone to other headsets in your talk group.

Remember Your group decides who can hear you. Your voice is never sent back to your own headset.

Point out that the host routes audio locally. Members hear only people in their configured talk group; separate groups stay isolated.

No route yet?

A paired headset is not always ready for voice audio. One headset by itself has no intercom route.

A double beep confirms that the microphone route is ready. Pairing alone does not guarantee that duplex audio has connected.

FULL-DUPLEX 04 / 09

Know whether your microphone is open.

●

Always open

Your microphone is live whenever its route is ready. Speak normally.

●

Push to talk

Hold the headset’s Play/Pause button to speak. Release it to mute.

If unsure Ask the host operator which mode is configured before you start.

In always-open mode, a ready microphone transmits continuously. Push to talk mutes until the headset button is held.

One queue per group Separate groups can have different people talking at once.

Explain that holding the button keeps the request queued. The double beep signals that the floor is theirs; releasing cancels a wait or ends a turn.

THE RIGHT CONTROL 07 / 09

Use Play/Pause, not call or answer.

▶Ⅱ Media Play/Pause

The headset button used for push to talk.

×Call or answerThese buttons do not activate push to talk.

×Keyboard buttonsTyping or pressing a key on the host is not push to talk.

!Three quick pressesIn full-duplex with PTT configured, this toggles your headset between push to talk and always open. Avoid it unless asked.

Stress the distinction between media Play/Pause and call/answer buttons. Three quick media-button presses can toggle modes when push to talk is configured.

QUICK CHECKS 08 / 09

If you cannot hear or be heard.

01

Check your button.

In push-to-talk or half-duplex, hold Play/Pause. In a queue, keep holding until the double beep.

02

Check the connection.

Ask the host operator to confirm your headset is connected and duplex audio is ready.

03

Reconnect, then try again.

After audio reconnects, release the button and press it again to request a turn.

Beep check The double beep confirms an active microphone route or your turn—not pairing by itself.

If problems remain after these checks, ask the host operator to inspect the connection and audio route before trying again.
