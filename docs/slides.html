A double beep confirms that the microphone route is ready. Pairing alone does not guarantee that duplex audio has connected.

Full-duplex 04 / 09

Know whether your microphone is open

●

Always open

Your microphone is live. Speak normally.

●

Push to talk

Tap Play/Pause to speak. Tap again to mute.

If unsure Ask the host operator which mode is configured before you start.

In always-open mode, a ready microphone transmits continuously. Push to talk mutes until the headset button is tapped to play.

  • Tap play once to queue.

One queue One person talks at a time.

Explain that one tap queues the request. The double beep signals that the floor is theirs; a pause tap cancels a wait or ends a turn.
